SERVICES

From first sample to final decision.

I

HELIUM EXPLORATION & SOURCING

Where it forms, how it moves, where it's trapped.

Helium source, migration and accumulation models for prospects and basins, built on noble gas isotopes, structural geology, heat flow and geophysics.

  • Play and prospect evaluation
  • Probabilistic targeting models
  • Fault and intrusion risk
II

NATURAL HYDROGEN EVALUATION

Separating real signals from artifacts.

Assessment of geologic H₂ occurrences, combining bulk gas and noble gas data with rock-crushing and fusion experiments that measure H₂ released from rocks.

  • H₂ source fingerprinting
  • Accumulation potential
  • Lab method design
III

CO₂ & CARRIER GAS SYSTEMS

The gas that moves the gas.

Quantifying CO₂–water interactions in aquifers (dissolution, precipitation, partitioning and migration) and their effect on helium and on CO₂ storage.

  • Solubility & mass-balance models
  • Gas–groundwater exchange
  • Fluid mixing & aquifer scale
IV

GEOCHEMICAL SURVEYS & DATA

Sampling plans that answer the question.

Design and leadership of soil-gas, well-gas and water sampling campaigns, plus interpretation of gas composition, stable isotope and noble gas data.

  • Soil-gas surveys
  • Lab selection & QA/QC
  • MATLAB / Python workflows
V

RESOURCE ASSESSMENT & MAPPING

A geologic basis for development.

Mineral and fluid resource assessments, from critical elements, precious stones and aggregate to helium, oil and gas, built in Petra and ArcGIS.

  • Structural & isopach maps
  • Well-log & cross-section work
  • Reservoir & production estimates
VI

DUE DILIGENCE & ADVISORY

Clear answers for decision-makers.

Independent technical review and commercial screening, including supply chains and midstream–downstream economics, written for boards, investors and tribal councils.

  • Technical due diligence
  • Commercial viability screens
  • Stakeholder briefings

APPROACH

Field, laboratory and model, connected end to end.

01FIELD

Plan and lead sampling campaigns: well gas, soil gas, water, core and outcrop. Collect the data the question needs, and nothing it doesn't.

02LABORATORY

Gas chromatography, isotope-ratio and noble gas mass spectrometry, with rigorous, reproducible methods and clear uncertainty.

03MODEL

Solubility, mass-balance, heat-flow and statistical models that turn measurements into source, migration and accumulation estimates.

04ADVISE

Plain-language findings, maps and recommendations that clients and communities can act on, with the limitations stated.

SELECTED WORK

Research and applied projects across the western US and Canada.

HELIUM · FOUR CORNERS

High-helium reservoirs of the Colorado Plateau

Navajo Nation, Four Corners area

Secured US $80,925 to fund noble gas isotopic analysis of helium wells. Identified the sources and controls on high-helium reservoirs to inform resource decisions.

Chemical Geology (2022) · doi
HELIUM · HEAT FLOW

How Yellowstone's heat releases crustal helium

Greater Yellowstone region

Combined noble gas isotopes and heat-flow modeling to show how a major thermal anomaly mobilizes helium from the crust, with direct implications for exploration near intrusions.

Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta (2025) · doi
EXPLORATION MODELING

Probabilistic targeting of helium-rich gas fields

Statistical exploration models

Quantified the role of faults and intrusions in helium-rich fields, and paired soil-gas geochemistry with geological and geophysical data to build statistical helium exploration models.

G-Cubed (2024) · doi  ·  Frontiers in Earth Science (2024) · doi
HELIUM + HYDROGEN · CANADA

He and H₂ exploration in Saskatchewan

Funded by the Saskatchewan Geological Survey

Integrating noble gas isotopic and bulk gas data with geologic and geophysical datasets to evaluate geologic hydrogen and helium accumulations (CAD $40,000, 2025–2026).

Manuscript in preparation · Earth and Planetary Science Letters
RESOURCE ASSESSMENT

Mineral and fluid resource assessments for tribal nations

BIA Division of Energy & Mineral Development, 2015–2024

CO₂ · CARRIER GAS

CO₂ as a carrier gas for helium

Caltech postdoctoral research

Quantified dissolution, precipitation, gas–water partitioning and fluid migration in aquifers to show how CO₂ strips and carries helium. This matters for both helium exploration and CO₂ storage.

Manuscript in preparation · Applied Geochemistry
